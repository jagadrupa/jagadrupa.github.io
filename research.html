Independent project · Public repository

Spatial niches in breast cancer

Visium spatial transcriptomics of invasive ductal carcinoma: manual niche annotation, then five supervised classifiers. Histogram gradient boosting reached 0.949 accuracy on a random split and a mean of 0.928 when whole tissue regions were held out.

Methods I developed and applied

Deep learning on sequencing signal

Variational convolutional networks in PyTorch for predicting methylation state and variants from nanopore and Illumina signal.

Graph and network models

Heterogeneous graphs linking genomes, metabolites and environmental variables, analysed with network propagation and community detection.

Multi-view integration

Metabolic modelling

MSc research project, University of Manchester, 2022; re-analysed in 2026

Congenital heart disease and kidney disease genetics

Rare-variant burden testing across 40 genes. The hypothesised link to chronic kidney disease was not supported (odds ratio 0.95, p = 0.68) and is reported as a negative result; the real signal is with kidney malformation (odds ratio 1.77, p = 3.1 × 10⁻¹³), pointing to a ciliary mechanism.
